**SurfArr** is self-hosted retro cable TV. It turns YouTube playlists and Internet Archive shows into channels that play around the clock, sorted by decade and genre. There's no on-demand: every channel runs on a virtual clock, so whenever you tune in something is already on, and everyone watching sees the same thing.

## What you get

- **TV channels for your media server.** SurfArr pretends to be an HDHomeRun tuner, so Plex, Jellyfin and Emby find it on their own and treat it like antenna TV, with a guide included.
- **An M3U playlist and an XMLTV guide** for any IPTV player.
- **40+ channels**: decade channels (70s Sitcoms, 90s Cartoons, 00s Kids…), themed channels (Nick at Nite, Fox Kids, Kids WB, Disney Afternoon, MST3K, G4…), 24/7 YouTube live streams, and any add-on packs you install.
- **A web dashboard**: a TV guide, channel settings, an add-on store, a way to report broken videos, and a full-screen CRT-style TV mode.
- **Curated content**: the channel lineup comes from the SurfArr API, already filtered so it's English only, with no compilations, reaction videos or short clips.
